Privacy Policy

obsidian-clone-lenovo is a private application used only by its owner.

The application accesses Google Drive in read-only mode in order to create a local copy of selected files for use with Obsidian.

Google Drive data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retrieved data is stored only on the owner's private computer. The application does not modify or delete data stored in Google Drive.

Access to Google Drive can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.
